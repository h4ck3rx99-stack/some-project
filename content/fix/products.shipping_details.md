# Offers include shipping details

`offers.shippingDetails` (OfferShippingDetails) tells agents delivery cost and time, a common shopper question.

## How to fix

Add `shippingDetails` with `shippingRate`, `shippingDestination` and `deliveryTime`, or configure shipping in Google Merchant Center as well.

## WooCommerce

Rank Math and Yoast WooCommerce SEO can output shipping details from your WooCommerce shipping zones; enable that option.

## How to verify

Look for `shippingDetails` in the Offer.

## Spec reference

Google Search Central “Product structured data” and schema.org `Product`/`Offer` (“Shipping details”).
